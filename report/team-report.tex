\section{Executive summary}

Nirmaan runs its startup-reimbursement process over email: teams send invoices and payment proofs, staff check them by hand, and approvals and payments are hard to track or audit. Neerman replaces this with one tracked workflow.

We shipped a server-rendered Spring Boot application (PostgreSQL, Thymeleaf/HTMX) with four role-specific work areas. Teams create claims and upload invoices and proofs. Staff verify them side by side. The COO approves, fully or partially, from a mobile-first screen. Finance records the payment with a UTR. Every transition is audit-logged and notified in-app and by email. We later added duplicate-invoice blocking and an MCP connector that lets ChatGPT or Claude prepare \emph{draft} claims; the student still presses Submit.

\section{Known limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
    \item Limitation 1 -- Single instance only: the login rate limiter and last-active throttle are held in memory, so running several app instances would not share lockout counts (needs Redis or similar).
    \item Limitation 2 -- Travel claims cannot be submitted: pre-approval categories are enforced but pre-approvals themselves are not built yet. Bank details and batch payouts are also missing, so Finance enters payments one claim at a time.
    \item Limitation 3 -- Emails are best effort: a failed SMTP send is logged, not retried, and there is no self-service password reset (staff send reset links). HEIC receipts upload but cannot be previewed in browsers.
\end{itemize}
