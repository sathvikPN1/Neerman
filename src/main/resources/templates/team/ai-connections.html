Use ChatGPT or Claude for your claims

Connect your AI assistant once. Then share a photo of a receipt or invoice and ask it to prepare the claim. It fills in the details, attaches the documents and gives you a link. You always review and press Submit yourself.

Connector URL

This app is running on a local address. ChatGPT and claude.ai need a public https address. Ask Nirmaan staff for the public link. Claude Code and Claude Desktop on this computer can use it as is.

How to connect

ChatGPT
  1. Open Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Create (custom connector), name it Nirmaan, paste the connector URL, choose OAuth.
  3. Sign in with your Nirmaan account when asked, and click Allow.
  4. In a chat, enable the Nirmaan connector and say: "Here's my invoice, prepare a Nirmaan claim."
Claude (claude.ai or the Claude app)
  1. Open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Nirmaan and paste the connector URL. Click Add, then Connect.
  3. Sign in with your Nirmaan account and click Allow.
Claude Code

Run this in a terminal, then type /mcp in Claude Code and choose Authenticate:

claude mcp add …
